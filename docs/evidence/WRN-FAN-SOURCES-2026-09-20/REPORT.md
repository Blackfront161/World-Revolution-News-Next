# Fan-source candidate review

**Disposition: directory-only candidates pending individual source passes.**

This pass records 18 official self-descriptions from six languages. It
separates explicit antifascist or revolutionary statements from general
anti-discrimination, supporter-rights, queer, feminist or community work. No
candidate is labelled anarchist, libertarian-communist or left solely from an
anti-discrimination statement. GARA is retained as a clearly marked
sport-adjacent political collective rather than a fan group.

The product directory integrates 14 new groups or networks after removing
the FARE duplicate already present in the existing nine-source directory and
holding Irrésistibles Français and GARA as research candidates pending a
more specific qualifying record.

The strongest explicit political candidates are Roter Stern Leipzig, Alerta
Network, Rebel Ultras, Clapton Community FC and Bukaneros. Progetto Ultrà and
Mondiali Antirazzisti provide the clearest organised fan/social-work evidence
in Italian. F_in, Queer Football Fanclubs and Football v Homophobia cover
women and queer supporters without an inferred broader ideology.

Official feeds were not asserted where a feed URL was not directly evidenced;
all `officialFeed` values therefore remain `null`. The research found no
equally reliable primary-source candidates in Greek, Russian or Turkish in
this bounded pass; those language gaps remain explicit in the JSON. No article
text, image, logo or stream was copied, and no candidate is a full-text or
media admission.
