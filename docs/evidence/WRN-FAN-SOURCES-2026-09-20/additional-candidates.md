# Additional fan-source gap check

Bounded primary-source review on 2026-09-20 found no additional candidate
that satisfies both requirements: an official source and an explicit
political self-description tied to a supporter group or sports collective.

- **Asteras Exarchion 1928 (Greek):** the official domain was inaccessible in
  this check. Search results were secondary reports or a video, so no
  admission claim is made.
- **Çarşı / Forza Beşiktaş (Turkish):** [the official supporter site](https://www.forzabesiktas.com/)
  confirms the group identity, Beşiktaş connection, 1982 origin and the
  motto “Birlik · Ruh · Mücadele”, but the accessible official text does not
  explicitly claim antifascism, antisexism, left politics or anarchism.
- **GARA (Portuguese):** [the official site](https://gara.pt/) explicitly
  describes an antifascist collective, yet the reviewed pages do not tie it
  to a supporter group or sports collective; it remains a research lead.

Therefore `additional-candidates.json` intentionally contains zero product
candidates. No political label is inferred from secondary descriptions.
